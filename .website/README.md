# Unifind

Unifind is a hidden-object game on a beat. One unicorn hides in a crowd of creatures dancing in time, in a nightclub or at an open-air party, and you have to spot it before the track ends.

<img src="https://github.com/LazyLuigi/unifind-js13k/raw/main/media/gameplay.gif" />

## How to Play
Find the Unicorn before the track ends.
Zoom in and out with the mouse wheel, or pinch the screen.


Playable on [wavedash](https://wavedash.com/games/unifind)
